How reminders reach you

  • While this calendar is open (a browser tab, or the installed app), every reminder fires on time with the style you picked: a notification, a silent notification, a ringing alarm, or a pop-up.
  • Reminders that came due while it was closed show up as missed the next time you open it (up to 12 hours back).
  • iPhone/iPad: Apple only allows web notifications from apps added to the Home Screen. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, open it from there, then turn notifications on.
  • Want reminders even when it's closed? Send your events to your phone's own calendar. The file includes every reminder, so your phone will alert you.
